---
name: ecommerce-ux-patterns
description: Pharmacy/e-commerce UX patterns for product grid, product page, cart, pricing display, promos, trust. Load when designing commerce flows.
---
- Grid tile: image, brand (muted), 2-line name, price (strike old price + discount chip), rating, stock state, quick-add. Out-of-stock dims + label, add disabled with reason.
- Product page: gallery with dots, price block with TVA note, stock pill, delivery promise, sticky CTA with qty stepper and total; "Added" confirmation + badge bump.
- Cart: line items with stepper + swipe delete + undo, TVA breakdown lines, free-delivery progress, clear total, empty state with CTA. Totals update instantly.
- Trust: clear prices incl. TVA, no dark patterns, no fake urgency/scarcity, medicines copy says "ask your pharmacist", no medical claims.
- Categories rail: icon + label, tap preselects category filter.
- Search: field is visual in Home header (search functionality is out of scope unless trivial; tapping goes to Category) — don't build a search screen (G-07).
